# Stock Ageing and Selling Priority
> Generated by `scripts/retail_generate_docs.py` from actual result files in `reports/ml/retail/` and `models/`.

`src/trexo/ml/retail/ageing/engine.py` (works only on ledger output).

## Categories (configurable `AgeingConfig`)
Fresh (weighted age <= 30 d) / Normal / Ageing (weighted age > 90 d, or oldest layer > 180 d with cover > 60 d) / Slow-Moving (cover > 180 d) / At-Risk (demand-risk >= 0.6) / Critical Dead-Stock Risk (>= 0.8) / **Confirmed Dead Stock** (verified stock > 0 AND risk >= 0.8 AND no sale for >= 90 days). The risk score is the existing calibrated recency rule, so the previous validation carries over. **The age/coverage thresholds are configuration defaults, not calibrated on real inventory (none exists)** - calibrate on pilot data.

## Calculated per product
Weighted and oldest stock age, last sale date, recent velocity (56 d), forecast demand/day, **days of cover**, ageing distribution (units by age bucket), units expiring within 30 days, minimum days to expiry, capital exposure at FIFO cost (unknown when any layer lacks cost; the known part is shown separately).

## Selling queue
`urgency = 0.35 risk + 0.25 age_factor + 0.25 cover_factor + 0.15 expiry_factor`; `priority_points = urgency x value_weight` (0.5-1.0 by capital-exposure percentile when cost is known). P1 >= 0.6, P2 >= 0.4, P3 >= 0.2. Weights are design choices needing owner sign-off. Fresh and Normal products are excluded from the queue so healthy stock is not pushed. Each row explains why to sell early, how urgent, remaining stock and cover, expected demand and the evidence.

Verified by unit tests (classification rules, queue ordering) and the synthetic demo; not yet run on any real shop.
