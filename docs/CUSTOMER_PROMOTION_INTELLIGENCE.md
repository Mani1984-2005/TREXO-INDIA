# Customer and Promotion Intelligence
> Generated by `scripts/retail_generate_docs.py` from actual result files in `reports/ml/retail/` and `models/`.

Data: 4,334 identified customers; 24.9% of lines have no CustomerID and are excluded. **Consent status of the source customers is unknown**, so all individual-level features are gated.

## Privacy controls (tested)
Pseudonymised IDs (salted SHA-256 prefix); aggregate segment reporting with k-anonymity suppression (< 10 customers); individual-level recommendations raise `PermissionError` unless `consent_verified=True`; saved customer models contain no customer-level data.

## Promotion responsiveness
