# TREXO Dataset Research Register

## Objective
Build a reliable India-focused retail forecasting dataset.

## Sources
1. M5 Walmart - retail forecasting benchmark.
2. UCI Online Retail - transaction data.
3. AGMARKNET - Indian wholesale market prices.
4. Consumer Affairs - Indian retail commodity prices.

## Strategy
Combine public datasets, clearly labelled synthetic kirana data, and future pilot-shop data.

## Validation
Use chronological splits, prevent data leakage, and evaluate MAE, RMSE and WAPE.

## Status
Research started. Data acquisition pending. Model training not started.
