# Profit Optimisation
> Generated by `scripts/retail_generate_docs.py` from actual result files in `reports/ml/retail/` and `models/`.

`optimization/profit_optimizer.py` assigns one price level per product with a mixed-integer linear program (`scipy.optimize.milp`). Products without cost are excluded (reported, not guessed).

## Strategies (explicit objectives)
| Strategy | Objective | Extra constraint |
|---|---|---|
| profit_max | maximise sum J | optional total discount budget |
| balanced | maximise sum J + 0.5 x capital released | optional budget |
| clearance_max | maximise units sold | sum J >= 95% of the profit_max J |

All use J from `SMART_PRICING_OPTIMIZATION.md` (profit, holding cost, obsolescence exposure) and only feasible (floor/limit-respecting) levels.

## Verification
Tested: one level per product, floors/limits respected, budget respected, strategy ordering (profit_max has the best J, clearance_max the most units within the 5% tolerance), and the MILP solution equals the **brute-force global optimum** on a small instance. The vectorised chooser used in simulation is tested equal to the reference implementation.

## Limits
Linear objective with fixed price levels; demand response is assumed/observational; single horizon; no multi-period replenishment, MOQ or supplier constraints; no real cost data, so **no profit-optimisation result on real business data exists**.
