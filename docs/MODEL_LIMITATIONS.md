# Model Limitations
> Generated by `scripts/ml_generate_docs.py` from the actual result files in `reports/ml/` and `models/`. Nothing here is hand-typed from memory.

1. **No stock data:** outputs are demand-inactivity indicators. Capital tied up, dead-stock value, excess inventory, recoverable value and carrying cost cannot be computed.
2. **One seasonal cycle (373 days):** seasonality can only be flagged; Q4 growth inflates 'Growing'; seasonal products appear dead out of season; recency calibration has no second-year check.
3. **ML adds nothing over recency here:** conclusions about drivers beyond recency are weak.
4. **Top-band overconfidence:** nominal score ~0.99 vs observed 0.84-0.88.
5. **Forecasts:** weekly only, <= 8 weeks, under-forecast bias in the Q4 ramp; WAPE 0.5-0.9 means individual product forecasts are rough; LightGBM not better than SES.
6. **Anomalies:** 6,244 transaction-quality flags (incl. 5,220 exact duplicates) and 3,810 demand-anomaly events are *candidates*, not verdicts; thresholds (x5 median, robust z>=6, x100 median, x10 price) are heuristics. Isolation Forest flags 78 product profiles, mostly very sharp recent trend changes.
7. **Product keys not case-normalised** (policy not approved); case variants are separate products.
8. **Duplicates retained** (no approved treatment); they inflate quantities slightly.
9. **Simulation is a unit-level scenario:** hypothetical purchases, no lead times/MOQ/carry-over/money; more purchase lines on zero-demand products under TREXO policy.
10. **No external validation:** a single UK wholesaler's data; not India-specific despite the project name. Generalisation to Indian kirana/retail is unproven.
11. **Not implemented because inputs do not exist:** transfers, supplier returns, customer-segment actions, lifecycle detection, location-level analysis.
12. **Stage movement artefacts contain the old D01 B row:** they are protected historical files and are not read by ML; use the D01-corrected copies in `data/ml/` for governance-clean movement.
13. **Not production ready:** no pilot data, no backend deployment, no security review, no monitoring.

## Data-readiness requirements for the next iteration
Stock snapshots (product x location x date), receipts and purchase orders, unit cost and selling-price margin, supplier terms (MOQ, lead time and its variability, return window), lifecycle status, returns linked to original invoices, >= 2 years of history.
