Zero-safe metrics: sMAPE defined with 0/0 = 0; WAPE rather than MAPE. Bias is (forecast - actual)/actual, negative = under-forecast (the Q4 ramp is not captured by lag-based methods).

**Findings.** SES has the lowest pooled MAE/WAPE; MA8 and LightGBM are within ~2% of it; Croston/SBA are clearly worse overall (they win only for 'erratic' series). The machine-learning model is therefore *not* better than simple smoothing on this data. Selected method per demand class (by backtest MAE): {'erratic': 'sba', 'intermittent': 'ma8', 'lumpy': 'ses', 'smooth': 'ses'}. Products with fewer than 4 active weeks use MA8 (documented fallback; low reliability).

Horizons: 1-4 weeks (short) and 5-8 weeks (medium) are evaluated; longer horizons are **not supported** by 53 weeks. ARIMA/SARIMA and seasonal decomposition were not used: one cycle cannot estimate seasonality and most series are intermittent. Output: `reports/ml/forecast_next_8_weeks.csv`.
