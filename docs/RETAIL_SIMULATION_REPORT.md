## Findings
1. In 15 of 16 scenarios the intelligence strategy placed **zero offers** and equalled the baseline: at assumed 40% margin and the validated elasticity, discounting lowers profit, and the engine protects margin by not discounting. That is the intended behaviour, but it also means **no benefit was demonstrated**.
2. Offers appeared only when elasticity was assumed to be -2 (decision=true e=-2.00: gross profit -371, units +202): slightly lower profit for a tiny sales gain.
3. The replay shows the multiplicative-response limit: leftover zero-demand units cannot be rescued by a 5-20% discount.
4. Stock/cost assumptions and write-off severity did not change the conclusion in the tested ranges.
5. The value of the system on this data is therefore in **detection, priority and not discounting blindly**, not in demonstrated discount-driven recovery. Replenishment prevention (not buying) was not simulated for lack of procurement data.

Not evaluated: stockouts, turnover under replenishment, expiry, location transfers, bundles (no economics), customer targeting.
