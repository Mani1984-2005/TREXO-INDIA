## Reorder (Module E)
`reorder_calc` implements z = Phi^-1(service level), sigma_LT = sqrt(L sd_d^2 + d^2 sd_L^2), safety = z sigma_LT, ROP = dL + safety, order-up-to = d(L+R) + safety, MOQ rounding, and **suppresses orders when dead-stock risk >= 0.6**. Unit-tested against hand-computed values. Without on-hand and lead time it returns `INSUFFICIENT_INPUTS`, `recommended_order_qty = None`, and demand-based guidance (DO_NOT_REORDER / PAUSE / REDUCE / DEMAND_SUPPORTS_REPLENISHMENT) with the expected 4-week demand. **No purchase quantity is ever invented.** Products flagged do-not-reorder at 2011-12-09 with non-LOW evidence: 484.
