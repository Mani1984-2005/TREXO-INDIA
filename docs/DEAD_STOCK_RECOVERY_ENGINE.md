# Dead-Stock Recovery Engine
> Generated by `scripts/retail_generate_docs.py` from actual result files in `reports/ml/retail/` and `models/`.

`src/trexo/ml/retail/recovery/engine.py`. Takes ageing-engine output, prices, optional costs, optional bundle evidence and demand patterns.

## Rules
- Fresh/Normal stock and Ageing/Slow stock that would sell out within 60 days of forecast demand are **not** touched ("do not discount what is selling").
- At-Risk and worse: stop/pause replenishment.
- Seasonal/campaign-concentrated demand: review seasonal timing before any discount.
- Bundle recommended only with co-purchase evidence.
- Controlled clearance offer only when cost, floor and the objective support it; otherwise "do not discount" or analysis only.
- Supplier-return review only for products the caller marks eligible; confirmed dead stock adds assortment review and management intervention.
- Not producible without data: inter-location transfers, supplier returns, customer targeting (listed in every row).

## Output per recommendation
Product id, remaining quantity, stock age, risk class, evidence, actions, suggested offer (if supported), price floor, expected quantity movement, estimated financial outcome (labelled computed-from-cost / assumed-response / not computed), confidence, `requires_management_approval = True` (always), outcome-measurement method.

**Ranking:** `urgency x feasibility x benefit_percentile` (feasibility 1.0 offer computable, 0.7 analysis/bundle, 0.5 non-price; benefit by capital at cost, else revenue at list price - labelled in `exposure_basis`).
**Confidence:** LOW without demand evidence or cost; MEDIUM with cost but observational/assumed elasticity; HIGH only for an experimentally validated price response (none exists).

Status: verified by unit tests and the synthetic demo (`demo_synthetic_recovery_plan.csv`: mostly "do not discount; improve visibility/cross-sell" because discounts do not pay under the estimated response). Not run on a real shop.
