# Product Bundle Intelligence
> Generated by `scripts/retail_generate_docs.py` from actual result files in `reports/ml/retail/` and `models/`.

Method: exact pair counting over invoice baskets (support, confidence both directions, lift), baskets of 2-60 distinct products (16,782 of 19,773 baskets), >= 15 co-occurrences. Pairs only: a two-product bundle is the executable offer, and FP-Growth/Apriori (mlxtend not installed) would add little for pair offers. Statistics are computed over multi-item baskets.

## Temporal validation (train before 2011-09-01, test after)
| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Directed rules with lift >= 1.5 and confidence >= 0.05 (train) | 23,437 |
| Share of those pairs that co-occur again in test | 88.6% |
| Share with test lift > 1 | 82.1% |
| Spearman correlation train vs test lift | 0.79 |
| Mean test confidence vs consequent base rate | 0.112 vs 0.035 (3.2x) |

Association patterns persist out of time. **This validates co-purchase, not that a bundle offer lifts sales or profit** - that needs a controlled test.

## Recommendations (sales-based, real data)
280 slow/at-risk/critical, non-seasonal, adequate-evidence products were considered; **54** have an anchor (a fast/medium product sold in the last 30 days with lift >= 1.5, confidence >= 0.05). Others get no bundle ("no incompatible clearing"). File: `reports/ml/retail/bundle_recommendations_sales_based.csv`. Example: BLUE NEW BAROQUE CANDLESTICK CANDLE with PINK NEW BAROQUECANDLESTICK CANDLE (lift 456.0).

## Economics
`bundle_economics` computes bundle price, discount cost and, **only when costs are supplied**, margin; it rejects bundles below approved floor, cost or minimum margin (tested). Without cost: `NOT_COMPUTED_COST_MISSING`. All real-data recommendations are therefore marked profit-unverified and approval-required.
