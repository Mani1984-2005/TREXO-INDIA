# Operational Data Requirements
> Generated by `scripts/retail_generate_docs.py` from actual result files in `reports/ml/retail/` and `models/`.

| Capability | Required data | Today |
|---|---|---|
| Stock balance, ageing, coverage | Ledger events (opening, receipts, sales, returns, damage, adjustments), per location | missing |
| Capital exposure, margin, floor, profit | Unit purchase cost (per receipt), selling price, approved minimum margin / price floor, maximum discount | missing (price exists) |
| Reorder quantity and timing | On-hand, open POs, supplier lead time and variability, MOQ, service level | missing |
| Expiry exposure | Expiry date per receipt | missing |
| Transfers | Per-location stock and demand | missing |
| Supplier returns | Return terms/windows | missing |
| Promotion effect | Promotion calendar, offer executed, control group | missing |
| Customer promotions | Consent records, purpose limitation | consent unknown |
| Seasonality | >= 2 years of history | 1 year |
| Calibration of ageing thresholds | pilot shop ledger >= 6 months | missing |

Record formats are defined by `ledger.EVENT_COLUMNS` and the feedback store schema. Minimum pilot: one shop, a verified opening count plus continuous event capture, costs on receipts, and approval-gated offers logged through the feedback store.
