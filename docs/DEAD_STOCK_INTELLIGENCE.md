# Dead-Stock Intelligence (Modules A and B)
> Generated by `scripts/ml_generate_docs.py` from the actual result files in `reports/ml/` and `models/`. Nothing here is hand-typed from memory.

## What the status means
No stock balances exist, so every classification is a **demand-inactivity / dead-stock-risk indicator**, not confirmed physical dead stock. `Confirmed Dead Stock` is only assignable when `on_hand_qty > 0` is supplied together with Critical-level evidence of non-LOW quality (tested). Every output also carries `status_type`, `evidence_basis`, `evidence_quality`, `evidence_flags`, `stock_verification_required` and `human_review_required`; inventory value is explicitly marked unavailable.

## How thresholds were selected, validated, calibrated
1. For each monthly cutoff (2011-03-10 ... 2011-06-08) every product with >=1 prior sale is labelled 1 if it has no valid sale in the next 60 days.
2. An isotonic regression of that label on *days since last sale* gives the transparent **risk score**. Recency at which the score first reaches 0.2/0.4/0.6/0.8 (final fit on all labelled cutoffs): **18 / 42 / 89 / 159 days**.
3. Class bands are cut on that probability (0.2/0.4/0.6/0.8) - interpretable and not tied to a universal day-count. All values are configurable (`ClassificationThresholds`, overwritten from `calibration.json`).
4. **Validation (out of time, train-only calibration):**

Observed inactivity rises monotonically with band in both periods. Caveat: in the top band the nominal score (~0.99) overstates the observed rate (0.84-0.88); read "Critical" as "roughly 85% of look-alikes had no sale in 60 days". The training range ended at ~189 days of recency; final calibration reaches 313 days and products beyond it carry `RECENCY_BEYOND_CALIBRATED_RANGE`.

## Module B - risk prediction
Target: no valid sale in next 60 days. Models: logistic regression, random forest, histogram gradient boosting, LightGBM; tuned by purged forward-chaining CV inside the training period; compared with the recency-rule baseline. Details and metrics: `MODEL_EVALUATION_REPORT.md`. **Outcome: no ML model beat the one-feature calibrated rule by the pre-set margin, so the rule is the deployed predictor.**

Outputs per product: id, description, classification, risk score, days since last sale, historical quantity, velocity, explanation, intervention hint, evidence quality (`reports/ml/dead_stock_classification.csv`).
